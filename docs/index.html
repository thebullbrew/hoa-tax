The method. Dues compound monthly off your escalation rate; opportunity cost compounds annually at your rate on every dollar paid — that's the "real tax" honest number, because money spent on dues is money not compounding somewhere else. Special assessments are added flat in the year they land. Dues escalation near inflation is normal; anything above 5%/yr sustained means a board funding deferred maintenance with your wallet. And the standing filter: if the numbers only work because you pretend the dues won't rise, they don't work.

For illustration only — not financial, tax, or legal advice. HOA budgets, reserves, and special-assessment risk vary wildly by building; read the resale package, not the listing copy.
